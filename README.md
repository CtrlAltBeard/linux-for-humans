## **📖 About This Series**
Linux doesn’t have to be scary. Whether you’re a complete newbie, a curious Windows/macOS user, or just someone who wants to **stop crying over terminal errors**, this series is for you.

Here, you’ll find:
- **Quizzes** to help you find your perfect distro.
- **Step-by-step guides** for installing, customizing, and troubleshooting Linux.
- **A touch of humour** to keep things light.
