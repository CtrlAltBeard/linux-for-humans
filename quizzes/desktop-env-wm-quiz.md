## **📜 Introduction: Find Your DE/WM Soulmate**

Tired of staring at a wall of Linux options? This **90s magazine-style quiz** will help you find your perfect **desktop environment (DE)** or **window manager (WM)** match—no PhD in computer science required.

Grab a pen and paper (or just tally in your head), and let’s get started.

## **❓ The Quiz**

### **1. How do you like to organize your workspace?**

- **A)** I want everything in its place automatically. I don’t want to think about it.
- **B)** I’ll arrange it myself, but I want it to look pretty.
- **C)** I don’t care how it looks; I just want it to be *fast*.
- **D)** I want to control everything with my keyboard. Mouse? What’s that?

### **2. How do you feel about your mouse?**

- **A)** I love my mouse. I couldn’t live without it.
- **B)** I use it, but I wouldn’t miss it if it disappeared.
- **C)** I tolerate it, but I’d rather not rely on it.
- **D)** I hate it. Give me keyboard shortcuts or give me death.

### **3. What’s your hardware like?**

- **A)** New and powerful. I’ve got RAM to spare.
- **B)** Decent, but not a beast.
- **C)** Old and slow. It struggles with modern websites.
- **D)** I don’t know, but I want it to run *fast*.

### **4. What’s your aesthetic preference?**

- **A)** Sleek and modern. I want it to look like a Mac.
- **B)** Customizable and unique. I want to tweak everything.
- **C)** Minimal and functional. I don’t care about looks.
- **D)** I want animations and eye candy.

### **5. How do you feel about learning curves?**

- **A)** I want it to work out of the box. I don’t have time for this.
- **B)** I’ll learn if I have to, but I’d prefer it to be easy.
- **C)** I enjoy a challenge. Bring on the config files.
- **D)** I live for the struggle. The harder, the better.

### **6. What’s your main use case?**

- **A)** Everyday tasks: browsing, office work, media.
- **B)** Development, coding, or creative work.
- **C)** Reviving old hardware.
- **D)** Gaming or multimedia.

### **7. How do you feel about system resources?**

- **A)** I have plenty. I don’t worry about it.
- **B)** I want a balance between looks and performance.
- **C)** I need something *lightweight*. Every MB counts.
- **D)** I don’t care, as long as it’s fast and responsive.

### **8. What’s your spirit animal?**

- **A)** A golden retriever: friendly, reliable, and easygoing.
- **B)** A fox: clever, adaptable, and stylish.
- **C)** A cheetah: fast, efficient, and no-nonsense.
- **D)** A chameleon: always changing, always adapting.

## **🔍 Your Results**

### **🥇 Mostly A’s (8-16 points): The Beginner’s Best Friend**

**Your Match:** **GNOME, KDE Plasma, or Cinnamon**

- **Why?** You want a **polished, user-friendly** experience with minimal setup. These DEs are stable, widely supported, and designed for ease of use.
- **Desktop Environment:** GNOME (modern, minimal), KDE Plasma (customizable, feature-rich), or Cinnamon (familiar, Windows-like).
- **Best For:** Everyday tasks, beginners, users who want a "just works" system.
- **Try It On:** Fedora (GNOME), KDE Neon (Plasma), Linux Mint (Cinnamon).

**Famous User:** Linus Torvalds uses **GNOME** (sometimes). If it’s good enough for the creator of Linux, it’s good enough for you.

---

### **🥈 Mostly B’s (16-24 points): The Tinkerer’s Sweet Spot**

**Your Match:** **XFCE, Budgie, or MATE**

- **Why?** You want **customization and control** without sacrificing usability. These DEs are lightweight but still user-friendly.
- **Desktop Environment:** XFCE (lightweight, fast), Budgie (modern, minimal), or MATE (traditional, stable).
- **Best For:** Users who want to tweak their setup but don’t want to dive into config files.
- **Try It On:** Xubuntu (XFCE), Ubuntu Budgie (Budgie), Ubuntu MATE (MATE).

**Famous User:** Linux Mint users *swear by* Cinnamon for its balance of simplicity and customization.

---

### **🥉 Mostly C’s (24-32 points): The Speed Demon**

**Your Match:** **Openbox, LXQt, or i3**

- **Why?** You prioritize **speed and efficiency** over eye candy. These are **lightweight** and fast, even on older hardware.
- **Desktop Environment/Window Manager:** Openbox (stacking, minimal), LXQt (lightweight DE), or i3 (tiling, keyboard-driven).
- **Best For:** Older hardware, users who want a no-frills experience.
- **Try It On:** Lubuntu (LXQt), AntiX (Openbox), Arch Linux (i3).

**Famous User:** Arch Linux users often start with **i3** for its minimalism and keyboard-driven workflow.

---

### **🏆 Mostly D’s (32-40 points): The Power User**

**Your Match:** **Sway, Hyprland, Niri, or AwesomeWM**

- **Why?** You want **full control** and don’t mind a steep learning curve. These WMs are **highly customizable** and often keyboard-driven.
- **Window Manager:** Sway (tiling, Wayland), Hyprland (dynamic, eye candy), Niri (scrollable workspaces), or AwesomeWM (dynamic, Lua scripting).
- **Best For:** Developers, power users, and anyone who loves tweaking their setup.
- **Try It On:** Arch Linux (Sway, Hyprland, Niri), or any distro with Wayland support (Niri).

**Famous User:** The **ricers** of r/unixporn use these to create their *aesthetic* setups.

---

## **🎯 Mixed Results?**

If your points are spread across multiple letters, you might be a **hybrid user**! Here are some combinations:

- **A + B:** **MATE or Deepin** – A traditional DE with a balance of simplicity and customization.
- **B + C:** **XFCE or LXQt** – Lightweight but still user-friendly.
- **C + D:** **i3 or Bspwm** – A tiling WM that’s lightweight but powerful.
- **A + D:** **KDE Plasma** – Customizable but still polished and user-friendly.

## **💡 Final Advice: Stop Overthinking It**

*"Unfollow DistroWatch, mute the Linux subreddit for a week, and just pick something. Seriously. Install Fedora with KDE, or Ubuntu with GNOME, and use it for 6 months before you even think about trying something else."*

**The Linux ecosystem’s beauty isn’t that you can try everything—it’s that you can find one thing that’s genuinely perfect for you and never feel pressured to upgrade it.**
